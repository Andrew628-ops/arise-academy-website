About Arise Academy

We are committed to academic excellence, character development, and a safe, nurturing environment for children.

School History

Arise Academy began as a small community school and has grown over the years into a respected institution in the region, focused on providing quality early years and primary education.

Mission

To provide a safe and stimulating learning environment that fosters curiosity, academic success, and strong moral values.

Vision

To be a leading primary school recognized for excellence, innovation, and the development of responsible young citizens.

Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Excellence
  • Respect
  • Discipline
  • Creativity
  • Responsibility

Our Journey

  1. 2008 — School founded
  2. 2012 — First graduating class
  3. 2018 — New science and ICT labs opened
  4. 2024 — Expanded facilities and improved curriculum
